Quick answers

I bought Pro, but the app says Free.
Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

I have a code. Where do I use it?
Open Settings in the app and tap Redeem a code.

My lists aren't showing up on my other device.
Before Tomorrow syncs through your own iCloud. Check that both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account and that iCloud is turned on for Before Tomorrow (Settings → your name → iCloud). New changes can take a minute to appear.

Will I lose anything if Pro ends?
No. Nothing gets deleted. You just can't add more than the free limits.

How do I cancel my subscription?
In the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Or in Before Tomorrow, go to Settings → Manage subscription.

There are more answers inside the app, under Settings → Questions and answers.
