Before Tomorrow

Privacy policy

Your lists are yours.

Last updated 28 November 2026

The short version: What you write stays yours. Nobody but you can see your lists, tasks or habits. The app counts a few anonymous things, like how often a task gets ticked, to see which features people use. There's no account, no ads and no ad tracking.

What you put in the app

Your lists, tasks, habits and history are stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on, they're also kept in your own private iCloud database (Apple's CloudKit), so they sync between your iPhone, iPad and Mac and come back if you reinstall. That database belongs to your Apple Account. The developer of Before Tomorrow can't see it, open it or download it.

If iCloud is off, everything stays on that device only.

Purchases

Before Tomorrow Pro is bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment, and no payment details are shared with the app or its developer. The app checks on your device whether you have Pro. There's no server involved.

Anonymous usage stats

To learn which features are worth improving, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count a few actions: opening the app, creating a list or habit, ticking a task, opening Review, seeing the Pro screen and buying Pro. Each count can say whether a task repeats or belongs to a habit, or which kind of list was made. It never includes what you typed: no task, list or habit names.

Along with those counts, Firebase receives an app-instance ID (a random number that resets if you delete the app), general device details like model and iOS version, a rough location worked out from your internet connection (country or city, never a precise location), and, if you buy Pro, which product and its price. None of this is linked to your name, email or Apple Account.

Advertising features are switched off. The app doesn't use your advertising identifier, doesn't ask to track you, and doesn't share data for ads. Google's handling of this data is covered by the Firebase privacy information.

What's not collected

No name, email, precise location, contacts or advertising identifiers, and none of the words you write in the app. Nothing is ever sold.

Apple may show the developer anonymous, combined numbers (like how many people downloaded the app) and crash reports from people who chose to share them with developers in their device settings. These can't be linked to you.

If you send an email

If you write in, your email address and message are used only to reply to you. They're never added to a mailing list or passed on.

Children

Before Tomorrow doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children. The anonymous usage stats above are the same for everyone.

Deleting your data

Delete a task, list or habit in the app and it's gone from your devices and your iCloud. To remove everything, delete the app and, if you like, its data in iCloud (Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Before Tomorrow).

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
